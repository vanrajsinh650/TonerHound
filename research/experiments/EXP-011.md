## 1. Executive Summary & Root-Cause Diagnosis

Prior to EXP-011, TonerHound achieved **Candidate Recall@5 of 96.36%** on ExtractBench, but **Candidate Recall@1 dropped to 59.60%** — a **36.76 percentage point resolution gap**. Over **69% of all benchmark failures** were caused by **wrong-occurrence competitor selection** on the correct document.

EXP-011 introduces the **Structural Evidence Reranker** (`StructuralReranker` in `src/tonerhound/resolution/reranker.py`), which augments local lexical and spatial scoring with five extractor-independent geometric signals:
1. **Page & Section Context** ($W_{\text{page}}$): Anchors candidates to verified container pages and penalizes off-target page term flooding.
2. **Horizontal Column Rail Corridor** ($W_{\text{column}}$): Aligns numerical and text tokens to established table columns and column peer consensus.
3. **Same-Row Band Co-Linearity** ($W_{\text{row}}$): Restricts candidate selection to the horizontal band ($|\Delta y| \le 0.015$) of the active record anchor.
4. **Sibling Anchor Locking** ($W_{\text{sibling}}$): Binds low-entropy scalar values directly to high-entropy record identifiers.
5. **Array Sequence & Row Monotonicity** ($W_{\text{sequence}}$): Enforces monotonic top-to-bottom record ordering ($y_{i-1} < y_i < y_{i+1}$) in structured repeating arrays.

## 3. Diagnostic 20-Case Ablation Study

Evaluated across the 20 curated hard-ambiguity diagnostic failure cases (`scratch/curated_20_cases_r5_vs_r1.json`):

| Ablation Configuration | Candidate Recall@1 | Successful Flips | Regressions | MRR | Delta MRR |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Baseline (No structural reranking)** | 0.0% | 0 / 20 | 0 | 0.450 | +0.000 |
| **+ Page Context Only** ($W_{\text{page}}=15$) | 35.0% | 7 / 20 | 0 | 0.658 | +0.208 |
| **+ Column Rail Only** ($W_{\text{col}}=8$) | 40.0% | 8 / 20 | 2 | 0.664 | +0.214 |
| **+ Same-Row Only** ($W_{\text{row}}=10$) | 0.0% | 0 / 20 | 0 | 0.450 | +0.000 |
| **+ Sibling Proximity Only** ($W_{\text{sib}}=8$) | 35.0% | 7 / 20 | 0 | 0.662 | +0.212 |
| **+ Array Sequence Only** ($W_{\text{seq}}=5$) | 0.0% | 0 / 20 | 0 | 0.450 | +0.000 |
| **Full Combined EXP-011 Reranker** | **85.0%** | **17 / 20** | **0** | **0.925** | **+0.475** |

### Key Diagnostic Takeaways:
1. **Orthogonal Synergy**: Neither Column Rail alone (40%) nor Sibling Proximity alone (35%) nor Page Context alone (35%) resolves the ambiguity space. Combining them yields an **85.0% Recall@1 flip rate (17/20 successes)**.
2. **Zero Regressions**: In isolation, column rail introduced 2 regressions due to unconstrained vertical jumps. When combined with page and sibling constraints, **regressions dropped to exactly 0**.
3. **MRR Doubling**: Mean Reciprocal Rank on hard cases increased from **0.450 to 0.925**.

## 4. Architectural Implementation Details

The implementation is encapsulated in `src/tonerhound/resolution/reranker.py`:
- `StructuralReranker`: Class implementing configurable decomposed scoring weights:
  - `w_column = 8.0`
  - `w_row = 10.0`
  - `w_sibling = 8.0`
  - `w_sequence = 5.0`
  - `w_page = 15.0`
- `ExtractionInput`: Extended in `src/tonerhound/models/types.py` with structural hints (`column_corridor`, `column_peers`, `row_corridor`, `sibling_boxes`, `expected_row_y`, `prev_row_y`, `next_row_y`, `target_page`, `page_confidence`, `is_header`).
- `EvidenceResolver`: Extended in `src/tonerhound/resolution/resolver.py` to route candidate lists through `reranker.rank_candidates()`.
- `ExtractBenchAdapter`: Extended in `src/tonerhound/benchmark/adapter.py` to propagate table column positions, previous/next row anchors, and sibling bounding boxes dynamically during resolution.
- Test Suite: 11 unit and adversarial tests added in `tests/test_structural_reranker.py` (100% passing).
