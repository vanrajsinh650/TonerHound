## 1. Executive Summary & Core Hypothesis

### The Problem
On the official ExtractBench benchmark, TonerHound's candidate generators achieve:
- **Candidate Recall@5**: **96.36%**
- **Candidate Recall@1**: **59.60%**
- **The Resolution Gap**: **36.76 percentage points**

Over **69% of all benchmark failures** are "wrong occurrence" errors on the correct page: the candidate generators find the true physical evidence box inside the top 5 candidates, but local text scoring picks a non-matching competitor (e.g. repeated `$200`, `0.00%`, `N/A`, `TX`, repeated dates or names across different rows/records).

### The Hypothesis
> **Hypothesis**: Replacing naive local scoring with an extractor-independent **Structural Evidence Reranker** will significantly improve Candidate Recall@1 (from ~60% toward 75%+) across repeated values by conditioning candidate selection on:
> 1. Horizontal Column Alignment (column corridor priors derived from verified sibling cells)
> 2. Same-Row Consistency (vertical line-band proximity to record anchors)
> 3. Sibling Anchor Locking (binding low-entropy scalars to high-entropy record keys)
> 4. Row-Pitch Continuity (enforcing top-to-bottom row ordering without backward jumps)
> 5. Page & Section Context (routing candidates to verified table and section boundaries)
> 
> This will directly lift Word Grounding F1 on the benchmark without degrading precision or introducing template-specific rules.

Five specialized subagents are currently analyzing the structural signals across failure sets:
- **Agent A (Column/Rail Analysis)**: Formulating relative horizontal corridor alignment scoring.
- **Agent B (Row/Record Consistency)**: Modeling same-row vertical proximity and sibling anchor locking.
- **Agent C (Page/Section Context)**: Formulating page and section containment without text similarity drift.
- **Agent D (Rank-2/3 Failure Audit)**: Auditing the 20 diagnostic cases where Recall@5 succeeds but Recall@1 fails.
- **Agent E (Evaluator)**: Building and validating the offline 20-case evaluator and 32-doc test harness.
